# Taste file of Granny Weatherwax (a fictional fixture)

This file collects the recorded verdicts of Granny Weatherwax on written and visual work for the Lancre Gazette, between
October and November 2026. Every rule here comes from something she said; the quotes are her words.

## Facts and claims

Every claim, number and description must match the source: the ledger, the minutes, the register.

- 2026-11-02, almanac post: "the almanac does not predict the weather. it records what the weather did". A claim about what a
  thing does must be what it does.
- 2026-11-05: a count of 335 sold copies included returned ones; the real figure was 105. A number that mixes returns with
  sales is wrong.

## Writing

### What they reject

- AI tells, above all sentences built to sound quotable. 2026-11-04: "this reads like a machine wrote it. One press does what
  would otherwise be a dozen hand-set pages, and only what matters is printed. That is such a tell." Other forms of it:
  - "X, not Y", "it's not A, it's B", "not just X";
  - "does what would otherwise take N", "one X instead of many";
  - groups of three; short sentences for effect; a sentence that announces the next one.
- Clickbait. 2026-11-06: "please stop writing like a clickbait pamphlet". A title states the point. No teasers.
- Post titles that sound machine-written. 2026-11-07, of "Four ways to read the almanac": "titles like that are a bit ai sloppy".
  A title names the subject and says plainly what it does or what was found.
- Corporate buzzwords. 2026-10-20: "no claude speak in the gazette please, nobody talks like that".
- Rules attributed to her that she did not make. 2026-11-08: "dont put words in my mouth. I did not make that rule".

### What they have accepted

- 2026-11-09, the harvest notice: "yes, plain and short, that is what I want".

## Visual work

### What they reject

- Colours turned down. 2026-11-10: "no teal anywhere near the masthead". 2026-11-12: "and drop the red one as well".
- Red and green together. 2026-11-11: "I have red green colour blindness, so never tell pass and fail apart by those two".
  Shape, position, weight or text must carry every meaning.
- Decorative glyphs. 2026-11-12: "those diamond thingies before each heading are kind of meh, and pills everywhere".
- Headings that shout. 2026-11-13: "tone the headings down, they are shouting at the reader".
- Images on the wrong ground. 2026-11-14: "that logo has a white background on a dark page".

### Motion and video

- A clip has to earn its place. 2026-11-15: "we dont need a video of the bees, we need the honey figures".

### What they have accepted

- 2026-11-16, the new masthead: "that one works, plain letters on dark".

## The shape of the work

Decide the shape before polishing anything, and ask in one line when it is not obvious:

- what goes in the post and what goes in the guide;
- whether each section needs text, a table, a chart or a clip;
- for a logo or a visual direction, three quick sketches that differ in idea.

Settle the logo and colours before making anything that uses them. On 2026-11-17 the logo was decided after the clips were made,
and every clip had to be recoloured.
